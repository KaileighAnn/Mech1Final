THE PROJECT

Meet the Feeder.

01 / STORAGE

Food Storage

A storage container holds dry dog food and directs it toward the dispensing mechanism.

02 / CONTROL
ARDUINO

Arduino Control

An Arduino reads a sensor and controls a servo motor to operate the sliding gate mechanism.

03 / DISPENSING
DIXIE

Automatic Feeding

The servo moves a rack-and-pinion sliding gate, allowing food to travel down a chute and into the bowl.

MECHATRONIC SYSTEM

How It
Works.

The Arduino receives input from a sensor, processes the information using a control algorithm, and commands the servo motor to open or close the food dispensing gate.

Design
Challenges.

Gate Jamming

Dog food could become stuck in the sliding gate. I will test and adjust the opening and clearances.

Servo Strength

The servo may not have enough torque to move the gate. I will test it under different food loads.

Portion Consistency

I will test different gate opening durations to improve the consistency of the food dispensed.

Sensor Reliability

I will test the sensor readings and adjust the detection threshold to avoid incorrect operation.
