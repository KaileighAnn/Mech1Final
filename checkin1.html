Check-In 01.

Mechatronics I Final Project | November 23, 2026

Check-In Overview

Since completing my project proposal, I have continued developing the mechanical components of my automatic dog feeder. My main focus has been preparing the sliding gate mechanism and planning the integration of the Arduino, sensor, and servo motor.

Mechanical Design Progress

I sourced an existing rack-and-pinion gate mechanism and modified it in SolidWorks to fit the opening of my food storage container. The servo motor will rotate the pinion gear, causing the gate to slide open and closed.

Manufacturing Progress

The gate components were prepared in PrusaSlicer for FDM printing. This will allow me to test the fit and movement of the mechanism before integrating the electronics.

  • Printer: Prusa CORE One
  • Material: PLA
  • Nozzle Diameter: 0.4 mm
  • Layer Height: 0.20 mm
  • Estimated Print Time: 1 hour 47 minutes

Electrical Components

The electrical system will use an Arduino to receive information from a sensor and control the position of the servo motor.

  • Arduino
  • Servo motor
  • Bowl detection sensor
  • Breadboard
  • Jumper wires
  • Power supply

Sensor and Servo Testing

The goal of this stage is to verify that the Arduino can receive input from the sensor and control the servo motor before connecting it to the sliding gate.

Testing Objectives

  • Verify that the sensor detects the bowl.
  • Verify that the servo rotates correctly.
  • Determine the required servo rotation.
  • Test communication between the components.
  • Prevent food dispensing when the bowl is absent.

Challenges and Design Considerations

Gate Clearance: The printed components must have enough clearance to slide freely without excessive movement.

Servo Rotation: The servo must rotate far enough to completely open and close the gate.

Food Jamming: The gate and chute must allow dry dog food to pass through without becoming stuck.

Sensor Placement: The sensor must be positioned where it can reliably detect the food bowl.

Next Steps

Before my next check-in, I plan to complete the sensor and servo testing, assemble the sliding gate mechanism, and begin integrating the mechanical and electrical components. I will also test the gate with actual dog food and make adjustments as needed.

Lessons Learned

So far, I have learned how to modify an existing mechanical design to fit my project. I have also learned the importance of considering how the mechanical and electrical components will work together before completing the final assembly.
