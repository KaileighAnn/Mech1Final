Project Proposal.

Mechatronics I Final Project | November 18, 2026

Project Overview

For my final project, I am designing an automatic dog feeder using an Arduino, a sensor, and a servo motor. The goal is to dispense dry dog food into a bowl automatically.

A servo motor will rotate a pinion gear that moves a sliding gate. When the gate opens, food travels down a sloped chute and into the bowl.

Minimum Viable Project (MVP)

  • Use an Arduino to control a servo motor.
  • Use a sensor to detect the food bowl.
  • Automatically open and close the sliding gate.
  • Dispense dry dog food into the bowl.
  • Write Arduino code to control the system.

Stretch Goals

  • Add an RTC module for scheduled feeding times.
  • Add a second sensor to detect low food levels and activate an LED indicator.

Required Components

  • Arduino
  • Servo motor
  • Bowl detection sensor
  • Rack-and-pinion sliding gate
  • Food storage container and chute
  • Frame and supports
  • Breadboard and jumper wires
  • Power supply
  • Optional RTC module and LED

Motivation & Learning Goals

I chose this project because next semester I will have a heavy class schedule, making it difficult to always be home to feed my dog, Dixie Mae, at consistent times.

I have already started designing the mechanical components in SolidWorks for another class. I want to expand the project by incorporating electronics and programming.

My goal is to gain experience with Arduino programming, sensors, servo motors, and integrating mechanical and electrical systems.

Project Schedule

  • Nov. 18: Complete proposal and component selection.
  • Nov. 23: Demonstrate sensor and servo operation.
  • Nov. 30: Integrate the gate and test food dispensing.
  • Dec. 6: Complete documentation and final demonstration.

Risks & Solutions

Gate Jamming: Food could become stuck in the sliding gate. I will adjust the opening and clearances if needed.

Servo Strength: The motor may not have enough torque. I will test the mechanism with small amounts of food first.

Inconsistent Portions: The amount dispensed may vary. I will test different gate opening durations.

Sensor Reliability: The sensor may incorrectly detect the bowl. I will test and adjust the detection threshold.
